Our story

Everyone has someone: the boss who takes the credit, the classmate who never stops laughing, the teacher who loves surprise quizzes.

Our hero has no face and no name — because the hero is you. Armed with a slingshot and a freezer full of ice cream, you take your sweet, harmless revenge, one splat at a time.
