Privacy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Hitscoop is a game by Swiff Studio. This page explains plainly what information the game uses.

In short

  • No account, no sign-up: the game never asks for your name, email or photo.
  • We have no servers and we do not collect any personal information ourselves.
  • The game is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob.

What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, Scoops, special ice creams and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them too.

Ads

  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your ad consent with Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Permissions.
  • On iPhone and iPad the game asks for tracking permission. If you do not allow it, you will still see ads, just less personalized ones.

Purchases

There are no real-money purchases in the game. Scoops are earned by playing, with the daily gift or by choosing to watch an ad.

Children

Hitscoop is meant for ages 12 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

This website

hitscoop.com uses no cookies, has no forms and does not track visitors.
