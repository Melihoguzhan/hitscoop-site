Privacy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Hitscoop is a game by Swiff Studio. This page explains plainly what information the game uses. Controller: Swiff Agency Prodüksiyon Televizyon Sinema Sanayi Ticaret Ltd. Şti. (Swiff Studio), Esentepe Mah. Feza Sok. No: 8 İç Kapı No: 3, 34394 Şişli / Istanbul, Türkiye, info@swiff.com.tr.

1. In short

  • Hitscoop has no separate account or sign-up; the game never asks for your name, email or photo.
  • We have no servers; we do not collect or store personal data about you.
  • The game is free and shows Google AdMob ads. If you like, you can buy Scoop packs; Apple or Google takes the payment.

2. What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, Scoop, special ice creams, settings, the names you type in Two Scoop and the transaction IDs of completed purchases (so the same payment is never granted twice) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them too.

3. Account connection and cloud backup

  • Before buying, or whenever you like from Settings, you connect your Google Play Games account on Android or your iCloud account on iPhone and iPad. No password or email reaches us; Google or Apple handles the sign-in.
  • While connected, a copy of your save (progress, Scoop, items, settings) is stored in your account, in Google Play Games Saved Games or in iCloud. We cannot see this copy; Google and Apple process it under their own privacy rules.
  • When you connect Google Play Games, Google lets the game know your player ID and player name; the game uses them on your device only to find your backup and does not send them to us.
  • To delete the backup: on Android, delete Hitscoop data in Google Play Games settings; on iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete Hitscoop.

4. Purchases

Payments for Scoop packs are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your card and payment details never reach us. The game only learns from the store which pack you bought and a transaction ID, and uses them on your device to deliver your Scoop and never grant the same payment twice. The stores keep your purchase history under their own rules.

5. Ads

  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads. Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your ad consent with Google's consent form; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → About → Ad Permissions.
  • On iPhone and iPad the game asks for tracking permission. If you do not allow it, you will still see ads, just less personalised ones.

6. Children

Hitscoop is meant for ages 12 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Your rights and contact

You have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection under the GDPR, Turkish KVKK and similar laws. Since we hold no personal data about you, most requests are met by deleting the data on your device (by deleting the app) and the cloud backup (with the steps above). For data held by Google and Apple, use their privacy centres. Questions and requests: info@swiff.com.tr. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

8. This website

hitscoop.com uses no cookies, has no forms and does not track visitors. As with any web request, the host technically sees your IP address; we do not collect or use it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date, and we announce important changes in the game.

  • Yedeği silmek için: Android'de Google Play Oyunlar ayarlarından Hitscoop verilerini, iPhone'da Ayarlar → [adın] → iCloud → Hesap Depolama Alanını Yönet bölümünden Hitscoop'u silebilirsin.

Scoop paketlerinin ödemesini Apple App Store ya da Google Play alır. Kart ve ödeme bilgilerin bize hiç gelmez. Oyun, mağazadan yalnızca hangi paketi aldığını ve bir işlem kimliğini öğrenir; bunları Scoop'u teslim etmek ve aynı ödemeyi iki kez vermemek için cihazında kullanır. Mağazalar satın alma geçmişini kendi kurallarına göre saklar.

  • Reklamları Google AdMob gösterir. Reklamı göstermek, ölçmek ve dolandırıcılığı önlemek için Google cihazının reklam kimliğini, IP adresini, cihaz ve uygulama bilgilerini ve reklamlarla etkileşimini kullanabilir. Ayrıntılar: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ya da İsviçre'deysen oyun Google'ın izin formuyla reklam iznini sorar; kararını istediğin an Ayarlar → Hakkında → Reklam İzinleri'nden değiştirebilirsin.

Hitscoop 12 yaş ve üzeri içindir ve 13 yaşın altındaki çocuklara yönelik değildir. Çocuklardan bilerek bilgi toplamayız.

2. What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, Scoop, special ice creams, settings, the names you type in Two Scoop and the transaction IDs of completed purchases (so the same payment is never granted twice) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them too.

3. Account connection and cloud backup

  • Before buying, or whenever you like from Settings, you connect your Google Play Games account on Android or your iCloud account on iPhone and iPad. No password or email reaches us; Google or Apple handles the sign-in.
  • While connected, a copy of your save (progress, Scoop, items, settings) is stored in your account, in Google Play Games Saved Games or in iCloud. We cannot see this copy; Google and Apple process it under their own privacy rules.
  • When you connect Google Play Games, Google lets the game know your player ID and player name; the game uses them on your device only to find your backup and does not send them to us.
  • To delete the backup: on Android, delete Hitscoop data in Google Play Games settings; on iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete Hitscoop.

4. Purchases

Payments for Scoop packs are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your card and payment details never reach us. The game only learns from the store which pack you bought and a transaction ID, and uses them on your device to deliver your Scoop and never grant the same payment twice. The stores keep your purchase history under their own rules.
